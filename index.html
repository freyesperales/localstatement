LocalStatement

PDF bank & card statements → clean CSV / Excel. No upload, no limits, no account.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your statement is parsed on this device with pdf.js — nothing is uploaded to any server, and no account is needed. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads.

How it works

  1. pdf.js reads the PDF in this tab and extracts every text fragment with its x/y position.
  2. Fragments are clustered into rows (by y) and columns (by x). A date at the left and amounts at the right mark a transaction.
  3. Three layout profiles are scored and the best-fitting one is auto-selected; you can override it above.
  4. If a balance column exists, each step is checked against the amount — mismatches are flagged so you can fix them in the editable table.
  5. Export clean CSV or Excel. Everything happened locally.
LocalStatement · v1 targets digital (text-layer) PDFs, not scans. The editable preview is your safety net — always reconcile before use.
